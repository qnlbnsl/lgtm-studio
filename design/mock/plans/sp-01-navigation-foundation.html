SP-01 Navigation foundation

Goal

The owner moves between repositories, projects, teams and agents, and the page they return to is where they left it.

What the owner sees

The sidebar lists each repository with its projects under it. Each repository opens and closes on its own.

Control K opens a switcher that finds a project, a team or an agent by name.

A thread keeps its unsent draft and its scroll position while the owner looks at another page.

Checks

The browser tests open three threads in turn and return to the first. Its draft and its position are unchanged.
