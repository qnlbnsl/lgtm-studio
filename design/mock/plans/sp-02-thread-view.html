SP-02 Thread view

Goal

A long reply stays readable. The thread opens at the latest message, and the draft survives a reload.

What the owner sees

A thread opens at its newest message. A "Latest" button appears when the owner scrolls up, and it returns to the end.

Several tool calls in a row fold under one line that says how many ran.

A large item, such as a long file, shows its first lines and loads the rest on request.

Checks

A thread of 4,000 messages opens in under one second. The browser tests scroll up, send a message, and see the position held.
