## Mechanisms reconsidered

The save join was wrong in two independent ways. It could trust the retained projection after disconnect and wait indefinitely for a replacement. The current snapshot is now the only completion evidence, with a finite wait and a retry that reuses known identifiers. The same request key still recovers a lost reply. The handoff now distinguishes the frozen three-mode driver from the deployed owner observation. The sample transition keeps the single workspace check and applies it before each delayed writer, including writers outside the sample screen that scheduled them.
